# Mark Anthony M. Kramer, Ph.D.
*HCI Researcher | Green AI & Computer Vision | Sustainable Sociotechnical Systems*

---

### About Me

I am a Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) researcher, system designer, and educator specializing in **Green AI**, **Green Computer Vision**, and **More-than-Human (MtH) interaction design**[cite: 1]. Holding a Ph.D. in Digital & Analytical Sciences from the University of Salzburg, my research bridges digital transformation with ecological responsibility, focusing on how intelligent computational systems can function within global planetary boundaries[cite: 1].

Currently serving as a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Institute for Computational Medicine (Vetmeduni Vienna), I design energy-efficient computer vision systems, hardware sensors, and sustainable precision livestock technologies deployed in real-world settings[cite: 1]. My work combines empirical user research, physical computing, and socio-ecological impact assessment to move interactive systems beyond traditional screens and foster meaningful well-being for human, non-human, and environmental actors[cite: 1, 3].

---

### Core Research Focus

* **Green AI & Energy-Efficient Vision:** Optimizing real-time computer vision pipelines for resource-constrained edge devices and low-energy environments[cite: 1].
* **More-than-Human Interaction:** Designing sociotechnical interfaces that incorporate non-human agents, animals, and broader ecological context[cite: 1].
* **Precision Animal Health & Sensors:** Developing non-invasive, sensor-based technologies for livestock care and field-based observation[cite: 1].
* **In-situ & Ethnographic HCI:** Utilizing 360° observation, situated action frameworks, and participatory design to evaluate technology in natural settings[cite: 1].
